Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

⚠️ This is a starting-point template, not legal advice. Review and adapt it with qualified counsel (and confirm your obligations under laws such as GDPR/CCPA) before relying on it.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Serviceflow ("we") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Serviceflow platform and related services.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — name, work email, organization name, and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Customer Data — the operational data you enter (customers, equipment, work orders, quotes, invoices, notes, attachments).
  • Usage & technical data — log data such as IP address, request metadata, and timestamps used for security, debugging, and reliability.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, secure, and improve the Service.
  • To authenticate users and prevent abuse (e.g., rate limiting sign-in attempts).
  • To send transactional messages such as status updates, quotes, and password resets.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share information only with service providers that help us operate the Service (e.g., hosting, email delivery, payment processing) under appropriate confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

4. Data retention

We retain Customer Data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. You may request export or deletion, subject to legal retention requirements.

5. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, role-based access control, and audit logging. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. To exercise them, contact your Serviceflow administrator or us at the address on your order form.

7. Cookies & local storage

The app uses browser local storage to keep you signed in (an access token) and to remember preferences such as theme. It does not use third-party advertising cookies.

8. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time; material changes will be communicated through the Service or by email.
