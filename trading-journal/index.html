How it works

This journal is wired straight into your Robinhood account. It pulls your realized profit & loss trade history and the underlying price series, then plots every closed trade on the chart where it happened — green triangles for winners, red for losers, sized by dollar impact.

To refresh with your latest fills, run python import_robinhood.py (see the README). It regenerates data.js and the whole journal updates.
